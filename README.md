## Our Philosophy: The Pedagogy of Wonder

*Designing discoveries. Cultivating perception. Honoring play.*

At Yonderfield, we believe that learning begins with wonder.

Wonder is more than awe or surprise. It is an invitation to inquiry—the moment something unfamiliar becomes not merely mysterious, but worth understanding.

We believe the most meaningful discoveries are not simply explained to learners. They are experiences learners arrive at through exploration, experimentation, and their own curiosity.

**Our work is to design the conditions in which those discoveries become possible.**

The Pedagogy of Wonder is an educational philosophy developed by educator and creative technologist Lenin Comprés through years of teaching, storytelling, creative experimentation, and learning alongside students and colleagues.

The Pedagogy of Wonder invites educators to become designers of discoveries: cultivating environments where learners feel free to question, play, make mistakes, pursue their fascinations, and develop richer ways of seeing the world.

**Yonderfield was founded to put this philosophy into practice**, bringing its principles into schools and communities through creative learning experiences, while continuing to explore, refine, and expand them through collaboration with educators and learners.

### Five Commitments

**1. Awaken Wonder.** Capture attention through beauty, mystery, humor, surprise, and play. Make discovery feel within reach.

**2. Design Discoveries.** Create experiences in which learners uncover meaningful relationships for themselves, transforming information into insight.

**3. Celebrate Absurdity.** Make room for experimentation, mistakes, unconventional ideas, and the freedom to look foolish.

**4. Trust Fascination.** Let learners' genuine interests guide their exploration, encouraging ownership and sustained curiosity.

**5. Expand Perception.** Help learners recognize patterns, relationships, and possibilities they could not previously see.

These commitments are not a prescribed sequence or rigid method. They are principles that inform the design of learning experiences, the relationships between educators and learners, and the environments in which discovery can flourish.

At Yonderfield, we seek to bring these ideas into classrooms and communities through creative, playful, inquiry-driven educational experiences.

### About the Founder

**Lenin Comprés (Lenino)**  
*Educator · Creative Technologist · Storyteller*

Lenin Comprés is a multidisciplinary educator, artist, and creative technologist whose work brings together storytelling, interactive media, play, and learning.

A professor at NYU's Tisch School of the Arts and a graduate of Columbia University's Teachers College, he has spent years exploring how creative experiences can cultivate curiosity, critical thinking, and meaningful discovery.

His work spans interactive technology, theater, game design, and educational experiences. Across these disciplines, he has developed a common approach: rather than simply communicating ideas, he creates opportunities for audiences and learners to discover them for themselves.

This approach led him to articulate **The Pedagogy of Wonder**, an educational philosophy centered on designing discoveries, encouraging experimentation, honoring play, and expanding perception.

He founded Yonderfield to bring the Pedagogy of Wonder beyond his own classroom, creating opportunities to explore, test, and develop its principles through educational programs and collaborations with schools and communities, beginning in Washington Heights, New York.
